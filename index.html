About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Statistics, advised by Prof. Ning Zhu, at the School of Management, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), and I expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests lie at the intersection of statistics, optimization, and machine learning, with particular interests in robust optimization, biostatistics, and quantitative finance. I have experience in mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, and computational implementation, including developing optimization models, designing statistical methods, and translating mathematical formulations into practical algorithms and code.

I plan to apply for Ph.D. programs in Fall 2026, with interests in Operations Research or Biostatistics.
