Working Paper · 2026In Progress

Distribution Estimation and Routing Optimization for Stochastic Service Times with Contextual Information

Service times in maintenance, emergency response, and home-service routing are often strongly related to observable context such as task attributes, customer status, region, and weather. This project estimates the conditional distribution P(S | X = x) using parametric, location–scale, and potentially nonparametric methods, then embeds the resulting uncertainty model into stochastic, robust, or distributionally robust routing formulations. Out-of-sample experiments compare route quality, constraint violations, and tail risk across sample sizes, context strength, distributional forms, and correlation structures.

Working Paper · 2026In Progress

MultiScale CenterQuery-CELLME

A supervised, patient-inductive framework for converting an expert-defined cellular microenvironment ontology into a reusable cell-level predictor. CELLME combines a center-aware local graph representation with multiscale cellular context and is evaluated across CRC, DLBCL, and NSCLC, including cross-domain transfer to an independent CRC CODEX cohort.

Policy Research Report · 2025Completed

Robust Surveillance-Camera Placement under Terrain Constraints

Developed a data-processing and optimization workflow for surveillance-camera placement using multi-year incident data, GIS visibility analysis, spatial coverage models, integer programming, and robustness considerations for camera reliability and terrain constraints. The final evaluation produced an approximately 40% reduction in system cost.

Patient Capital–Driven Development of Industrial Entities

Analyzed multi-year firm-level panel data to study how patient capital supports R&D investment, firm development, and innovation. The work used fixed- and random-effects panel regression, principal component analysis, ridge regression, and financial feature engineering in a high-dimensional, small-sample setting.

MCM/ICM · Problem C · 2026Meritorious Winner

Counterfactual Analysis of Voting Rules Using Constrained Bayesian Inference and Rejection Sampling

Built a constrained Bayesian framework with a truncated Dirichlet posterior and rejection sampling to infer latent weekly fan-vote shares. The paper then uses posterior counterfactual analysis, mixed-effects models, and an adaptive hybrid rule to study voting-rule sensitivity, uncertainty, and the balance between judges and fans.
